STEP 02 • PROBLEM & ONBOARDING

Pakistan Market Ki 3 Asal Kamiyan (Gaps) & Shop KYC

Gap 1: Naya Maal vs Phansa Maal

Daraz, Bazaar aur OLX par sirf naya ya B2C maal bikta hai. Har medical store, mart aur garments shop par 20%–30% sarmaya Dead-Stock ya Near-Expiry (2–3 months left) mein phans kar zaya ho jata hai.

Gap 2: WhatsApp & Parchi Ka Dhoka

Abhi dukandar majboori mein WhatsApp groups ya kabariye ko 70% nuqsan par maal dete hain. Wahan na koi price lock hota hai, na verification, aur har waqt fraud ka darr rehta hai.

Gap 3: Cash Ki Tang-Dasti (Barter Gap)

Chotay dukandaron ke paas naya stock lene ke liye cash nahi hota. StockGo par woh bina cash diye, apni dukan ka ruka hua maal de kar doosre dukandar se apne kaam ka maal Barter Swap kar sakte hain.

Module 1: Verified Shopkeeper Onboarding, RBAC & Trust Engine
1. 4-Role RBAC System Admin, Verified Shopkeeper, Bulk Liquidator (Lot Buyer), & Pending User.
2. Shop KYC Upload Dukandar apna Visiting Card, License ya Bill upload karega; Admin verify karega.
3. Contact Masking Phone number aur dukan ka naam tab tak chupa rahega jab tak 2% fee pay na ho.
4. 3-Strike Fraud Ban 2 dafa kharab maal spot par reject hone par Seller account aur CNIC Blacklist!

INSTRUCTOR QUESTION #01

"Dukandar wapas Supplier ya Company ko maal kyun nahi de deta?"

Market Fact: Pakistan ki wholesale markets (Bolton Market, Shah Alam, Medicine & Garments) mein 80% se 90% maal "Strict No-Return / One-Way Sale" policy par bikta hai!

01

Imported & Wholesale Lots

China, Dubai ya local wholesale markets se aane wale Electronics, Cosmetics, Garments aur Accessories ka koi official distributor nahi hota—woh 100% "Bika Hua Maal Wapas Nahi Hoga" ki parchi par milta hai.

02

Bulk Scheme / Target Stock

Companies dukandar ko lalach deti hain ke "100 cartons uthao to 15% extra scheme milegi, magar yeh stock Non-Returnable hoga." 60 cartons bik jate hain aur 40 dukan ke godam mein phans jate hain.

03

25% Katoti & 6-Month Delay

Jo ginti ki companies near-expiry wapas leti bhi hain, woh 25% deduction karti hain aur cash dene ke bajaye 6 mahine baad credit note deti hain, jabke dukandar ko aaj cash ya chalta hua maal chahiye.

04

Shop Closure / Category Shift

Jab koi dukandar apni dukan band kar raha ho ya winter se summer stock par shift ho raha ho, to pichle saal ke suppliers maal wapas nahi lete—StockGo un ka wahid sahara hai.

INSTRUCTOR QUESTION #02

"Kya pata Seller kharab, toota hua ya nakli (Defective) maal de de?"

Technical Solution (Modules 2 & 6): StockGo mein 4-Layer "Zero-Fraud Inspection & OTP Gate-Pass Engine" laga hai—jab tak Buyer apni aankhon se maal check kar ke Secret OTP na de, deal mukammal hi nahi hoti!

1. Live Batch & Expiry Photo

Seller Google se uthayi hui fake photo nahi laga sakta. React form mein usay carton par छपे Batch Number, Mfg Date aur Expiry Date ki asli clear photo upload karni hogi.

2. Quality Condition Grading

Seller ko qanooni taur par Grade chunna hoga:
🟢 Grade A: 100% Factory Sealed Carton.
🟡 Grade B: Minor Box Damage (Andar se product bilkul theek).

3. 4-Digit Secret OTP Gate-Pass

Buyer online Seller ko paise nahi deta. Woh Digital PDF Gate-Pass le kar dukan par jata hai, cartons khol kar check karta hai, aur tasalli hone par apna 4-Digit OTP Seller ko deta hai.

4. Spot Reject & Instant Refund

Agar maal photo se mukhtalif ya kharab nikle, to Buyer wahin "Reject Defective Stock" click karega—Buyer ki 2% wallet fee foran refund hogi aur Seller ko Fraud Strike lag jayegi!

INSTRUCTOR QUESTION #03

"Agar dukandar ki cheez StockGo par bhi Sell na ho to phir kya hoga?"

Technical Solution (Module 4): StockGo par maal ruka nahi rehta! Hamara Backend Cron-Job "3-Stage Auto-Clearance Rescue Funnel" har hafte khud-ba-khud agla step trigger karta hai:

STAGE 01 • DAY 7 TRIGGER

Auto Price-Drop & Push Alert

Agar 7 din tak kisi lot par offer na aaye, to system Seller ko 1-click +10% Extra Discount ka prompt deta hai aur us shehar ke 50 dukandaron ko "🔥 Hot Price Drop Alert" bhej kar feed ke top par le aata hai.

STAGE 02 • DAY 10 TRIGGER

Auto-Barter Priority Mode

Aksar maal cash na hone ki wajah se rukta hai. 10 din baad system us lot par "Barter Open — 0% Cash Needed" ka green highlight badge laga deta hai taake doosre dukandar apne ruke hue maal se foran adla-badli kar lein.

STAGE 03 • <15 DAYS LEFT

Bulk Liquidator Flash Pool

Jab expiry mein sirf 15 din reh jayein, to lot 60%–75% Off Flash Pool mein chali jati hai jahan Itwar Bazaar, Landa Market aur Village Wholesalers usay foran utha lete hain. 100% nuqsan ke bajaye 35% cash wapas!

100% Free Listing Guarantee ("No Sale, No Fee"): StockGo par maal lagane ki koi fee nahi hai. Agar kisi wajah se deal na bhi ho, to dukandar ki jeb se ek rupya bhi kharch nahi hota!
STEP 07 • MODULE 3 CORE LOGIC

"inDrive-Style" Fair Price Floor Lock & Smart Barter Calculator

1. Seller Guidance & Buyer 20% Floor Lock

Jis tarah inDrive mein gahak mazaq wali kam qeemat nahi likh sakta, StockGo ka backend Seller aur Buyer dono par Fair Price Lock lagata hai:

  • 🔵 Dead-Stock (No Expiry): System suggests 15% – 35% Discount.
  • 🟡 Urgent Expiry (30–90 Days): System suggests 30% – 50% Discount.
  • 🔴 Critical Expiry (<30 Days): System suggests 50% – 70% Discount.
Buyer Quick Bid Buttons (Asking Price: PKR 70,000):
-5% (66,500) -10% (63,000) -15% (59,500) -20% Floor Lock (56,000 🔒)
⚠️ PKR 56,000 se kam likhne par "Submit Offer" button disable ho jata hai!

2. Dual Engine: Cash Bid vs Barter Swap Math

Agar Buyer ke paas cash nahi hai, to woh "Offer Barter Swap" select kar ke apni dukan ki lot lagata hai aur Express Controller khud hisab barabar karta hai:

Seller Lot Value (50 Cartons Shampoo): PKR 1,00,000
Buyer Swap Lot (40 Cartons Soap): PKR 85,000
Auto-Calculated Difference (Top-Up Cash): Buyer Pays PKR 15,000 Cash + Stock

Barter mein bhi Buyer ki lot kam az kam 80% value ki honi zaroori hai.

STEP 08 • MODULE 5 MONETIZATION

StockGo Prepaid Wallet, Contact Masking & 2% Deal Unlocker

1. Contact Masking Middleware

Marketplace par har dukan ka naam aur phone number chupa hota hai (e.g., "Verified Medical Store - Gulshan, Karachi") taake Buyer aur Seller website se bahar rabta kar ke commission chori na kar sakein.

2. Prepaid StockGo Wallet

Har dukandar ke navbar mein Wallet Balance hota hai. Woh Dummy Card (Stripe Test) ya JazzCash / Raast / EasyPaisa screenshot upload kar ke balance mangwata hai jisay Admin 1-click mein approve karta hai.

3. 2% Fee Lock & Reveal

Jab Seller offer Accept karta hai, to deal Awaiting Commission ho jati hai. Jaise hi Wallet se 2% Fee kat'ti hai, dono ko Phone Number, WhatsApp button, Shop Map aur PDF Gate-Pass mil jata hai!
